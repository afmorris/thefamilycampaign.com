Dearest friends,

For hours now the household has sat in darkness, and for hours I have sat before the open wall, conducting what I can only describe as an interrogation. The wires, at long last, have confessed — and their testimony has shaken me.

I discovered a shared neutral running between two circuits entire. I discovered another, binding the fans to the shower light in an alliance no man sanctioned and no map recorded. Whoever wired this position before me fought by rules of his own devising, and I have spent the better part of a day deciphering his doctrine. I record it all faithfully, that no future soldier need suffer as I have suffered.

Nor was that the day's only reversal. My chosen ground for the new switch box proved already occupied — a toggle bolt, anchoring the shower's grab bar, entrenched precisely where I meant to dig. I was forced to withdraw and select new ground, and I do not mind telling you the wall bears the scars of my reconnaissance. The drywall shall demand its own campaign in due season. I have added it to the ledger of debts this house holds against me.

But friends — the plan is complete. Every wire is known, every neutral accounted for, every route drawn. The hour of reassembly is upon us. What was taken apart in confusion shall be put back in understanding.

Yours in hard-won intelligence,
— Cpt. Tony
