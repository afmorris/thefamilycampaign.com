Dearest friends,

Today I open a new front, and it lies not in some distant wood but within my own bedchamber. The master bathroom switch — once a loyal servant of this household — has turned traitor. It will summon the light, but it will not dismiss it. Such insubordination cannot stand.

The plan of battle is ambitious: a wholly new box shall be established to house the fan switches, a fresh garrison on new ground, while the old position is to be dug out and replaced with deeper fortifications. This is no mere skirmish. This is engineering.

The opening maneuver is complete. I have marched upon the armory and severed the enemy's supply lines — though I confess the campaign was not without collateral. In the course of my testing, fully half the household was plunged into darkness, and I ask that history record those brave rooms as necessary sacrifices, made in service of the greater cause.

The wires now lie dormant. The wall awaits. I go to open it up, and whatever I find within, I shall face it as a soldier.

Yours in darkness, by my own hand,
— Cpt. Tony
