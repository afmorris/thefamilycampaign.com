Dearest friends,

Light has returned to the household.

Every switch answers its calling. Every fan obeys. The traitor that once refused to dismiss the light has been drummed out of service entirely, and its successors — tested, proven, and true — now stand their posts. After seven hours of darkness, of interrogated wires and stripped screws and unmapped neutrals, the campaign is won.

One matter remains upon the ledger: the wall itself, which bears honorable wounds from the day's maneuvers. Tonight I shall dress them roughly, as a field surgeon does, and tomorrow the finer work of restoration begins. But that is tomorrow's campaign, and tonight I will not let it dim the hour.

I confess, friends, as I stood in the doorway flicking the switch on and off like a man who had never before beheld electricity — it was, in its small way, beautiful. This house asked something of me today, and I answered.

What an adventure indeed.

Yours in light, at last,
— Cpt. Tony
