Dearest friends,

Camp is established. The tents rose with admirable speed, though one pole required three attempts and a great deal of muttering. The hounds have assumed command of the cooler and refuse all petitions for its surrender. I judge it wise to let them have it.

A supper of sausages and beans was taken by lantern light. The river murmurs nearby like an old man with a long story. The youngest recruit has retired to the tent with a book and a flashlight, having declared the woods “actually pretty okay.” Praise does not come higher from that quarter.

Tomorrow we march to the falls. I shall report again after the reconnaissance.

Yours in triumph, — Cpt. Tony
